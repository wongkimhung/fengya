After nearly a year of preparation, Karfanjara's new plant in Tbilisi is now operational. The site adds automated profile cutting and corner-assembly lines, raising total capacity by roughly 40%.

More capacity means shorter survey-to-delivery lead times and the ability to take on larger commercial and residential projects. A new QC lab tests every batch for wall thickness, strength and surface finish — backing our 15-year warranty.

We thank every customer and partner for their trust. Next, the team focuses on colored profiles and lower-energy glazing.
