A local lifestyle outlet recently toured the Karfanjara workshop for a feature on how a window goes from aluminum profile to on-site install. The piece highlights our multi-chamber thermal-break structure, factory sealing and the 15-year warranty we offer owners.

In the interview, the team shared its "long game" philosophy: windows are low-frequency, long-life products. The real cost isn't the first quote — it's the airtightness, water resistance and efficiency a decade later.

We welcome visitors to the workshop to see how a window is made with care.
