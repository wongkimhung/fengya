Several European countries have tightened building-envelope requirements, making the window U-value a key factor for approval and subsidies. Thermal-break aluminum — with its multi-chamber insulation and recyclable metal — is now favored for both new builds and retrofits.

For owners, European-standard thermal-break windows cut heating loss in winter and cooling loss in summer. We recommend involving the window supplier early in design to avoid rework on opening sizes or styles.
