本项目为一栋位于第比利斯山地的独栋别墅，业主希望在不破坏山景视野的前提下，获得稳定保温与现代立面。我们提供了断桥铝平开窗、推拉门与玻璃护栏的整体方案。

从免费上门测量开始，工程师结合建筑的朝向与开窗节奏，确定了统一的型材颜色与开启方式。原厂完成型材加工后，本地团队在 3 周内完成全部 86 樘窗扇的安装与验收，并通过气密与防水测试。

bodyEn: |
  <p>For a hillside villa near Tbilisi, the owner wanted stable insulation and a modern façade without losing the mountain view. We delivered casement windows, sliding doors and glass railings as one system.</p>
  <p>After a free on-site survey, engineers set a single frame color and opening logic based on orientation and window rhythm. The factory finished the profiles; our local team installed and signed off all 86 leaves within three weeks, passing airtightness and water tests.</p>
