本项目要求在不影响办公租户的前提下，对一栋市中心办公楼层进行空间重构。我们采用玻璃隔断与铝合金扶手，在保持通透感的同时划分会议、协作与静音区。

为降低对日常运营的影响，安装团队全部采用夜间作业，并在每个施工日结束前完成现场清理。玻璃隔断搭配同色铝合金框体，与既有幕墙风格保持一致。

bodyEn: |
  <p>The brief was to reconfigure a city office floor without disturbing tenants. We used glass partitions and aluminum balustrades to divide meeting, collaboration and quiet zones while keeping the space open.</p>
  <p>To limit impact, the install team worked off-hours and cleared the site at the end of every shift. Color-matched aluminum frames keep the look consistent with the existing curtain wall.</p>
